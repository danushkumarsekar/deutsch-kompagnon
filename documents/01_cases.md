# German Cases (Die vier Fälle)

German has four grammatical cases. Every noun, article, adjective, and pronoun changes form depending on its case.

## Nominativ (Nominative) — The Subject

The Nominativ is used for the subject of the sentence — the person or thing doing the action.

**Question words:** Wer? (who?) / Was? (what?)

Examples:
- **Der Mann** liest ein Buch. (The man reads a book.)
- **Die Katze** schläft. (The cat sleeps.)
- **Das Kind** spielt im Garten. (The child plays in the garden.)

## Akkusativ (Accusative) — The Direct Object

The Akkusativ is used for the direct object — the person or thing directly affected by the action.

**Question words:** Wen? (whom?) / Was? (what?)

Article changes: der → den, die → die, das → das, die (plural) → die

Examples:
- Ich sehe **den Mann**. (I see the man.)
- Sie kauft **die Zeitung**. (She buys the newspaper.)
- Wir lesen **das Buch**. (We read the book.)

## Dativ (Dative) — The Indirect Object

The Dativ is used for the indirect object — the recipient or beneficiary of the action.

**Question words:** Wem? (to whom?)

Article changes: der → dem, die → der, das → dem, die (plural) → den (+ n on noun)

Examples:
- Ich gebe **dem Mann** ein Buch. (I give the man a book.)
- Sie hilft **der Frau**. (She helps the woman.)
- Wir danken **den Kindern**. (We thank the children.)

## Genitiv (Genitive) — Possession

The Genitiv shows possession or belonging. It's less common in spoken German but important in written and formal German.

**Question words:** Wessen? (whose?)

Article changes: der → des (+ s on noun), die → der, das → des (+ s on noun), die (plural) → der

Examples:
- Das Auto **des Mannes** ist neu. (The man's car is new.)
- Die Farbe **der Blume** ist rot. (The flower's color is red.)
- Das Ende **des Films** war traurig. (The end of the film was sad.)

## Quick Case Overview Table

| Case | Article (masc) | Article (fem) | Article (neut) | Article (plural) |
|------|----------------|---------------|----------------|------------------|
| Nom  | der            | die           | das            | die              |
| Akk  | den            | die           | das            | die              |
| Dat  | dem            | der           | dem            | den (+n)         |
| Gen  | des (+s)       | der           | des (+s)       | der              |

## When to Use Which Case

- Subject → **Nominativ**
- Direct object → **Akkusativ**
- Indirect object (usually "to/for someone") → **Dativ**
- Possession ("of the...") → **Genitiv**
- After specific prepositions → depends on the preposition (see preposition guide)
